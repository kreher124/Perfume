Make your own

  1. Make a Google Sheet with a tab called Tried. Put these headings in the first row: House, Name, Rating, Notes.
  2. Give each perfume a rating from 0 to 100. Leave the rating blank for ones you haven't tried yet. Those go in your queue.
  3. Color the House and Name cells green for any full bottle you own.
  4. Optional: add a To Try tab for your wishlist, with House, Name, Where to buy, Link, and Notes.
  5. Tap Share in Google Sheets and set General access to Anyone with the link.
  6. Copy the sheet's link and paste it below.
